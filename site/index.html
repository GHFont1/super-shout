🎙️
Listening…

Everything you love about dictation apps. None of the catches.

Speaking is 4× faster than typing. Super Shout makes it feel effortless.

⌨️

Hold-to-talk anywhere

Hold fn (or Right ⌘ / Right ⌥), speak, release. Text is inserted at your cursor in any app — no window switching, ever.

🙌

Hands-free mode

Quick-tap the hotkey to lock the mic open for long emails and documents. Tap again when you're done.

🧹

Automatic cleanup

Filler words removed, punctuation and capitalization fixed — instantly, on-device. Optional Claude AI polish for perfect grammar.

🧠

It learns how you talk

Correct a transcript once and Super Shout remembers. "you PC" becomes UPC, "a sin" becomes ASIN — permanently, and it biases the recognizer so it gets them right next time.

🚗

Knows what you mean

Say "2019 Genesis G7 brakes and rotors" and it types Genesis G70 — because there is no G7. Near-miss product and vehicle names snap to the real ones: CX 5 → CX-5, F150 → F-150.

🗣️

Spoken commands

Say "new paragraph" for a line break, "scratch that" to retake a sentence, and "press enter" to send the message — no keyboard needed.

🎯

Context-aware output

Search boxes don't get sentence periods, terminals get literal text, and Esc cancels a dictation before anything is typed.

🪄

AI Edit & AI Compose

Hold Right ⌘, say "make this more professional," and your selected text is rewritten in place. Hold Right ⌥ and describe what to write — a finished draft lands at your cursor. Your own Claude key, off until you add it.

📖

Vocabulary & dictionary

Feed it your acronyms, brands, and SKUs. "great call athletics" always comes out "Great Call Athletics."

✍️

Formats as you speak

Spaces and capitals land correctly when you continue a sentence, missing periods get added, and spoken lists turn into bullets.

🌊

The Shout Bar

A minimal floating pill shows a live waveform and your words as you speak — then gets out of the way.

⚡️

Native & instant

A real Mac app, not an 800 MB Electron shell. Launches instantly, idles at ~0% CPU, and works with no internet at all.

📞

Phone & meeting mode

Capture your microphone and computer audio as separate, labeled streams. Generate a summary and action items, then export clean Markdown.

🔎

Searchable transcript library

Find anything you dictated by word, app, date, or meeting summary. With an AI provider configured, ask questions across your recent history.

🧩

Snippets & automatic modes

Expand spoken shortcuts into reusable text and switch formatting rules automatically between email, chat, code, and your own app groups.

🔄

Updates itself

Signed updates are checked daily, verified cryptographically, downloaded in the background, and installed safely through Sparkle.

Private by architecture, not by policy

Other dictation apps stream your voice — and even screenshots of your screen — to the cloud. Super Shout doesn't.

🔒

On-device transcription

Speech recognition runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's neural engine. Your voice never touches a server.

📵

Works offline

Plane, dead zone, locked-down network — dictation keeps working because there's nothing to connect to.

👁️‍🗨️

Audio-only meeting capture

Meeting mode requests macOS Screen Recording permission only to receive computer audio. It does not save video or screenshots. Cloud AI features remain optional.

Up and running in one minute

Three permission prompts, then you never think about it again.

Download & open

Grab the app, drop it in Applications, and launch. If macOS says it "cannot be opened," right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again — it's a one-time step for apps outside the App Store.

Grant permissions

The welcome window walks you through Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Accessibility with live checkmarks. That's what lets it type anywhere for you.

Hold fn and talk

Click into any text field, hold the key, say what you're thinking, release. Done.
