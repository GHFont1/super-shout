# Super Shout 2.1.0

- Turns AI Do and AI Ask into movable, resizable native Mac mini-windows.
- Adds real close, minimize, and maximize controls, plus remembered window size and position.
- Adds typed and pasted follow-up prompts to AI Do.
- Adds drag-and-drop and paperclip attachments for files and folders in both AI Do and AI Ask.
- Keeps completed AI Do results visible without reopening a window you intentionally closed.
